# Module 4: Answers and Worked Solutions

حُرُوفُ الجَرِّ

Attempt each lesson before checking its answers. For a prepositional construction, check the particle’s class and meaning, the noun’s case, the attachment where required, and the contribution to the sentence. A correct translation alone is not a complete tarkīb.

The models use the classifications and analytical conventions taught in the lessons. Where a second analysis was introduced, it is identified as an alternative rather than marked wrong. A particle is **مبني لا محل له من الإعراب**; a fixed pronoun still has a case position determined by its role.

## Lesson 1

**1G**

1. **مجرور**: noun governed in جرّ. **تعلّق**: connection to a governor. **آلة**: instrument.
2. **كَتَبَ خَالِدٌ بِالقَلَمِ**. **القلمِ** is genitive by bā, with visible kasrah. Its instrument meaning does not make the noun accusative.
3. **كتبَ**: past, fixed on fatḥah. Its subject is **ضمير مستتر تقديره هو في محل رفع فاعل**. **الباء** is original; **الهاء** is fixed on kasrah in **محل جرّ بالباء**. **به** attaches to **كتب**, expressing an instrument. The phrase’s function and the pronoun’s internal case position must be distinguished.

**1I**

1. **جَلَسَ الضَّيْفُ فِي المَسْجِدِ**: **الضيفُ** is the nominative subject of **جلس**. **المسجدِ** is genitive by **في**. The phrase attaches to **جلس**, locating the sitting: **ظرف لغو، مفعول فيه غير صريح**. In **الضَّيْفُ فِي المَسْجِدِ**, **الضيفُ** is مبتدأ; the phrase attaches to an omitted general governor such as **كائنٌ** and supplies **في محل رفع خبر**: **ظرف مستقرّ**. The internal noun stays genitive in both.
2. Not every phrase expresses an object: it can express time, place, cause, instrument, or another relationship. The preposition governs its noun in جرّ regardless of an accusative relationship assigned to the whole phrase.

**1R**

**بِقَلَمِ المُعَلِّمِ**: **قلمِ** is genitive by bā and is مضاف; **المعلمِ** is genitive as مضاف إليه. Only the first noun is directly governed by the preposition. The whole phrase attaches to **كتب** as an instrument.

## Lesson 2

**2G**

1. **بالقلم**: آلة. **بالليل**: مفعول فيه للزمان. **بدرهم**: عوض.
2. **ذَهَبَ خَالِدٌ بِالطِّفْلِ**: **خالدٌ** is the subject, nominative with ḍammah; **الطفلِ** is genitive by bā, with kasrah. **بالطفل** attaches to **ذهب**: **الباء للتعدية، والجارّ والمجرور مفعول به غير صريح**. It means Khalid took the child away.
3. The phrase can specify taking firmly, a manner of the action, or describe the taker as being in a state of strength. Both readings preserve the noun’s genitive ending; they differ in what is described.

**2I**

1. **كَتَبَ المُعَلِّمُ بِالقَلَمِ. عَاقَبَ الطِّفْلَ بِذَنْبِهِ.** In the first sentence **المعلمُ** is the subject; **بالقلم** attaches to **كتب**, expressing an instrument. In the second, the subject is implicit **هو**, referring to the teacher. **الطفلَ** is the direct object. **ذنبِ** is genitive by bā and is مضاف; **الهاء** is in محل جرّ مضاف إليه, referring to the child. **بذنبه** attaches to **عاقب**, expressing cause: **مفعول له غير صريح**.
2. Accompaniment is a common implication of **باء التعدية**, not a condition of every occurrence. The construction can make the action reach its object without physical accompaniment.

**2R**

**سَافَرْتُ بِهِ بِاللَّيْلِ**: **سافرتُ** is a past verb fixed on sukūn before subject **تُ**, which is in محل رفع. **الهاء** in **به** is in محل جرّ بالباء, referring to the person taken along. The first phrase expresses the mediated object relationship; the second locates the travel in time. Both attach to **سافرت** through different relationships.

## Lesson 3

**3G**

1. **سِرْتُ مِنَ المَسْجِدِ إِلَى البَيْتِ**: from the mosque to the house.
2. **أوّلِ** is genitive by **من** and is مضاف; **النهارِ** is مضاف إليه مجرور. The phrase attaches to **عملت** and marks a starting point in time.
3. Cause: **من للتعليل، والجارّ والمجرور مفعول له غير صريح**.

**3I**

1. **خَرَجَ الضَّيْفُ مِنَ المَسْجِدِ. سَارَ إِلَى البَيْتِ.** Both verbs are past, fixed on fatḥah. **الضيفُ** is the subject of **خرج**, nominative with ḍammah. **من** governs **المسجدِ**; its phrase attaches to **خرج**, marking the starting place. The subject of **سار** is implicit **هو**, referring to the guest. **إلى** governs **البيتِ**; its phrase attaches to **سار**, marking the destination. Both phrases express place; neither supplies a direct accusative object.
2. **تائب** is an **اسم فاعل** with the verbal meaning “repenting.” It is a **شبه فعل** capable of governing the phrase completing that meaning.

**3R**

**القلمِ** is genitive by bā. **أولِ** is genitive by **من**, and **النهارِ** by إضافة. All show visible kasrah. In **إليه**, **الهاء** is fixed on kasrah **في محل جرّ بإلى**: positional inflection. The spelling change **إلى → إليه** does not make the pronoun accusative.

## Lesson 4

**4G**

1. **المجاوزة**: separation/passing beyond. **الاستعلاء**: being over/upon. **الإلزام**: obligation.
2. **جَلَسَ الطِّفْلُ عَلَى السَّرِيرِ**: **جلسَ** is past; **الطفلُ** is فاعل مرفوع بالضمة. **على** is original, expressing literal استعلاء; **السريرِ** is مجرور بعلى بالكسرة. The phrase attaches to **جلس** as **مفعول فيه غير صريح**. The bed locates the sitting; the verb does not act directly upon it as an object.
3. **عن** is a noun meaning **جانب** because it follows **من** in this construction. It occupies **محل جرّ بمن** and is مضاف.

**4I**

1. **نَهَيْتُ الطِّفْلَ عَنِ الشَّرِّ**. **نهيتُ**: past fixed on sukūn; **التاء**: subject in محل رفع. **الطفلَ**: direct object, accusative with fatḥah. **عن** is original, expressing separation; **الشرِّ** is genitive with kasrah. **عن الشر** attaches to **نهيت** as a mediated object relationship. The kasrah in **عنِ** is for connected pronunciation, not noun case.
2. If **ه** refers to the food, the phrase means giving while loving that food: accompaniment, **مفعول معه غير صريح**. If it refers to Allah, the phrase expresses giving out of love for Him: cause, **مفعول له غير صريح**. The same visible noun ending does not settle the pronoun’s referent or the phrase’s function.

**4R**

**جِئْتُ مِنْ عَنْ يَمِينِهِ**: **من** is the preposition; **عن** is a fixed noun in محل جرّ بمن, meaning “side,” and is مضاف. **يمينِ** is مضاف إليه مجرور and itself مضاف. **الهاء** is a fixed pronoun in محل جرّ مضاف إليه. The phrase beginning with **من** attaches to **جئت**, indicating where the coming began.

## Lesson 5

**5G**

1. **الكتاب لخالد**: ملك, ownership; the phrase supplies the predicate through an omitted general governor. **جئت للتعلم**: تعليل, intended purpose, attached to **جئت**. **قلت له اجلس**: تبليغ, the addressee, attached to **قلت**. The last two are not the same meaning merely because both follow verbs.
2. **المعلمِ** is genitive by kāf with kasrah. **كالمعلم** attaches to **كتبت**, expressing resemblance in the manner of writing: **مفعول مطلق غير صريح**.
3. They did not intend the resulting enmity and sorrow. **لام العاقبة / الصيرورة / المآل** fits.

**5I**

1. **جَلَسْتُ فِي المَسْجِدِ؛ كَتَبْتُ كَالمُعَلِّمِ؛ جِئْتُ لِلتَّعَلُّمِ**.
2. **وَهَبْتُ لِلطِّفْلِ كِتَابًا**. **اللام حرف جرّ أصليّ**; **الطفلِ اسم مجرور باللام وعلامة جرّه الكسرة**. **للطفل** attaches to **وهبت** and identifies the recipient, a **مفعول به غير صريح** relationship. **التاء** is the subject; **كتابًا** is the directly expressed object naming the gift.
3. Prepositional kāf takes an overt noun, **اسم ظاهر**, in the construction taught here. The attached-pronoun permission of bā does not transfer to it.

**5R**

**جِئْتُ إِلَى المَسْجِدِ لِلتَّعَلُّمِ. كَتَبْتُ فِيهِ بِالقَلَمِ.** **إلى المسجد** marks the destination; **للتعلم** marks purpose; both attach to **جئت**. **فيه** attaches to **كتبت**, locating the writing in the mosque; **الهاء** refers to **المسجد** and is in محل جرّ بفي. **بالقلم** also attaches to **كتبت**, but expresses an instrument. Every **تُ** is a subject-pronoun in محل رفع.

## Lesson 6

**6G**

1. **قمت الليل إلى نصفه**. The halfway point is not the final part of the stated night or something joined to its end.
2. The noun directly governed is overt **آخر**. The attached **ه** is governed by إضافة to that noun, not directly by **حتى**.
3. **سِرْنَا حَتَّى المَسْجِدِ**. **المسجدِ** is genitive with kasrah. **حتى المسجد** attaches to **سرنا** and marks the final limit of the journey, **مفعول فيه غير صريح**.

**6I**

1. **إلى نصفِه** selects an internal stopping point. **حتى آخرِه** reaches the final part of the named period. In both, the directly governed noun is genitive, is مضاف, and governs its pronoun as مضاف إليه.
2. It is an extreme of rank, **غاية في الرتبة للتعظيم**. Prophets are not a place reached by the action. With the specified prepositional analysis, **الأنبياءِ** is genitive by **حتى** with kasrah; its definite article permits kasrah here.

**6R**

Prepositional **حتى** takes an overt noun; in the time/place construction its limit must be a final part of what precedes or joined to that end. **إلى** can take a pronoun directly, **إليه**, and can mark an internal point such as **إلى نصفه**. Neither restriction should be replaced with a vague rule that the two words are always interchangeable.

## Lesson 7

**7G**

1. **باء القسم**: overt noun or pronoun. **واو القسم**: overt noun. **تاء القسم**: **الله** in this construction.
2. **متعلّق بفعل قسم محذوف تقديره أقسم**.
3. Meaning and construction decide the use. **بالقلم** in **كتبت بالقلم** names the instrument of writing and attaches to **كتبت**; it does not invoke an oath verb.

**7I**

1. For **أقسم والله**, use **أُقْسِمُ بِاللهِ**, retaining the expressed verb, or **وَاللهِ**, omitting it. For **تالعصر**, use **وَالعَصْرِ** or an oath with bā such as **أُقْسِمُ بِالعَصْرِ**; use **تاللهِ** if choosing the restricted tāʾ construction and changing the noun. These are grammatical repairs, each with its own noun sworn by.
2. **أُقْسِمُ بِاللهِ**: **أقسمُ** is فعل مضارع مرفوع بالضمة, with implicit subject **أنا** in محل رفع. **الباء**: حرف جرّ وقسم أصليّ. **اللهِ**: اسم مجرور بالباء بالكسرة. **بالله** attaches to **أقسم** as مفعول به غير صريح. In the oath expression **باللهِ**, the same governor is understood rather than expressed.

**7R**

Of the four listed, bā accepts an attached pronoun directly. Prepositional kāf, prepositional **حتى**, and oath wāw take overt nouns in the constructions taught here.

## Lesson 8

**8G**

1. **ما رأيته منذُ اليومِ**. The present period requires جرّ.
2. **ما رأيته مذْ يومانِ** is preferred for the elapsed past duration. The genitive **مذ يومينِ** is an allowed alternative, not the preferred form in that context.
3. **يومينِ** is dual: **اسم مجرور بمنذ، وعلامة جرّه الياء لأنه مثنّى**. The nūn’s kasrah is not the sign of جرّ.

**8I**

1. **مَا رَأَيْتُهُ مُنْذُ يَوْمِ الجُمُعَةِ. نِمْتُ مُنْذُ نِصْفِ اللَّيْلِ.** The first is past and negated; the second is past and affirmative but extended. **يومِ / نصفِ** are genitive by **منذ** and are مضاف; **الجمعةِ / الليلِ** are مضاف إليه. The phrases attach to **رأيت / نمت**, expressing time.
2. A complete clause follows, and a preposition does not govern a clause as though its first word were an ordinary noun. **منذ** is an اسم مضاف; the following clause is in محل جرّ بالإضافة. In **علمتُ الغيبةَ محرّمةً**, **التاء** is the subject, **الغيبةَ** the first object, and **محرّمةً** the second. The underlying assertion is **الغيبةُ محرّمةٌ**.
3. With the present period جرّ is obligatory; with the past, **مذ** favours رفع. For **مذ يومان**, understand a clause such as **مذ مضى يومان**. Do not call the nominative noun a genitive complement.

**8R**

**من أوّلِ الليلِ** names a beginning, directly governing **أول**. **إلى آخرِ الليلِ** names an endpoint, directly governing **آخر**. In both, **الليلِ** is genitive by إضافة. **منذ يومينِ** names an elapsed duration, directly governing the dual **يومين** by جرّ with yāʾ.

## Lesson 9

**9G**

1. **رجلٍ** has a visible genitive imposed by **ربّ**, but is the subject of the nominal predication: **مجرور لفظًا، مرفوع محلًّا على الابتداء**. The clause **لقيته** is its predicate.
2. **رُبَّ رَجُلٍ كَرِيمٍ لَقِيتُهُ**. Its overt complement is indefinite; **الرجلِ** does not meet that restriction.
3. **ما الكافّة** prevents **ربّ** from governing a genitive complement. **ربما** can therefore introduce **يصوم زيدٌ**, a verbal clause, with **زيدٌ** as the nominative subject. The expression still contributes frequency or quantity according to context.

**9I**

1. **رُبَّ طَالِبٍ مُجْتَهِدٍ لَقِيتُهُ**. **ربّ**: حرف جرّ شبيه بالزائد للتكثير، لا متعلّق له. **طالبٍ**: مجرور لفظًا بربّ، مرفوع محلًّا مبتدأ. **مجتهدٍ**: نعت مجرور بالكسرة. **لقيتُ**: past fixed on sukūn; **التاء**: subject in محل رفع; **الهاء**: object in محل نصب, referring to the student. **لقيته**: جملة فعلية في محل رفع خبر.
2. **خرج من البيت**: original **من**, beginning in place, attached to **خرج**. **عندي من ماء**: partitive **من**, “some water,” classified as شبيهة بالزائد; it supplies a partitive meaning without the separate verb attachment required by original **من**.
3. **العالمِ** is genitive by **حاشا** with kasrah. The particle expresses exclusion. **شبيه بالزائد** concerns the absence of a dependent verb attachment, not the absence of a specific meaning.

**9R**

**من للتعليل** gives a reason, such as **من الخوف**. **من للبيان** specifies the kind or substance, such as **من الدمع**. **من للتبعيض** means “some of,” as **من ماء**. The first two are original uses; the third is شبيهة بالزائد in the classification taught here.

## Lesson 10

**10G**

1. **مجرور لفظًا، منصوب محلًّا، وهو خبر ليس**.
2. **كتب بالقلم** has original bā expressing an instrument and a phrase attached to **كتب**. **هل زيد بقائم** has additional bā before the predicate; **قائمٍ** is genitive in form but nominative in position. There is no independent instrument relationship.
3. **من** is additional. It strengthens the question’s scope and governs the visible genitive, but does not create a separate dependent phrase requiring a verb attachment. **مزيد** occupies the مبتدأ position.

**10I**

1. **لَيْسَ الضَّيْفُ بِنَائِمٍ**. **ليس**: فعل ماضٍ ناقص جامد للنفي، مبني على الفتح، لا محل له من الإعراب. **الضيفُ**: اسم ليس مرفوع بالضمة الظاهرة. **الباء**: حرف جرّ زائد. **نائمٍ**: خبر ليس مجرور لفظًا بالباء، منصوب محلًّا. The visible ending is kasrah; the predicate position is accusative. No original bā attachment is supplied.
2. **سرت من البيت**: أصلية، لابتداء الغاية، متعلّقة بسرت. **عندي من ماء**: شبيهة بالزائد، للتبعيض، لا متعلّق لها. **هل من مزيد**: زائدة، لتأكيد العموم، لا متعلّق لها.
3. An additional preposition contributes emphasis and imposes the genitive form. What it does not add is a separate original-preposition relationship such as place or instrument.

**10R**

**بقلمٍ** with writing: original bā; the noun is genitive, and the phrase is an instrument attached to the verb. **ربّ رجلٍ … لقيته**: شبيه بالزائد; the noun is genitive in form and nominative as مبتدأ in position; **ربّ** has no dependent attachment. **ليس زيد بقائمٍ**: additional bā; **قائم** is genitive in form and accusative as خبر ليس in position, with no separate bā attachment.

## Lesson 11

**11G**

1. **الجحود**: reinforced negation. **التبيين**: clarification. **التقوية**: strengthened grammatical government.
2. **شيءٌ** is اسم ليس مؤخّر, nominative. **مثلِه** supplies the fronted predicate: **مثلِ** is مجرور لفظًا بالكاف الزائدة، منصوب محلًّا خبر ليس مقدّم; it is مضاف, and **الهاء** is مضاف إليه in محل جرّ.
3. No. It may clarify an object relationship, as the beloved after lām in **ما أحبّني لرسول الله**; that lām is original. The label identifies a clarifying use but does not by itself prove the additional classification.

**11I**

1. It is **لام التقوية**. The complement **للرؤيا** appears before its governor **تعبرون**; the lām strengthens that governing connection. On the additional analysis, **الرؤيا** retains the object position while being genitive in form; its final alif prevents a visible case vowel. Another analysis treats this strengthening lām as original.
2. **كالمعلم** in **كتبت كالمعلم** has original kāf, comparison, and attachment to **كتبت**, specifying the manner. In **ليس كمثله شيء**, kāf is additional, and **مثل** is the predicate in an accusative position; it does not form a separate original-kāf dependent phrase.

**11R**

All three classes can produce a genitive form. Distinguish them by specific meaning, attachment, and sentence function: original supplies a dependent relationship; شبيه بالزائد supplies specific meaning without that attachment; زائد supplies emphasis while the noun retains its sentence position.

## Lesson 12

**12G**

1. **قياسًا**: by a regular construction. **سماعًا**: established usage. **نزع الخافض**: removing the genitive governor. **تضمين**: incorporating another verb’s meaning.
2. **كَفَرُوا رَبَّهُمْ**: **ربَّ** is منصوب بنزع الخافض, with fatḥah; it is مضاف, and **هم** is مضاف إليه. **كَفَرُوا بِرَبِّهِمْ**: **الباء** is original; **ربِّ** is genitive by bā with kasrah and is مضاف; **هم** is مضاف إليه. The phrase attaches to **كفروا**. **واو الجماعة** is the subject in both. This omission is established, not an unrestricted new pattern.
3. **أنّي بريءٌ** has its own internal government: the speaker-pronoun is اسم أنّ in محل نصب; **بريءٌ** is خبر أنّ مرفوع. The whole noun-equivalent serves the outer complement relationship. An outer position is not transferred to every word inside it.

**12I**

1. **تضمين**: **تأكلوا** incorporates **تضمّوا**, “add/join,” whose **إلى** relationship explains **إلى أموالكم**. **تناوب**: **إلى** stands with the meaning of **مع**. Both yield the intended accompaniment; they differ in whether the explanation concerns the verb’s included meaning or a preposition standing for another.
2. Omission has specified regular environments and established exceptional expressions. Similar English translations do not grant identical Arabic government or permission to delete a preposition.
3. **من القوم** identifies those from whom the person hides. **من سوء ما بشّر به** gives the cause of hiding. The two phrases express different relationships and can therefore attach to the same **يتوارى**.

**12R**

**مجرور لفظًا منصوب محلًّا** retains a visible genitive form, as **قائمٍ** in **ليس زيد بقائم**. **منصوب بنزع الخافض** has an accusative form after the omitted preposition, as **ربَّهم**. In an ordinary noun with visible endings, expect kasrah in the first and fatḥah in the second; they are not two descriptions of an identical visible ending.

## Cumulative practice

### A. Meaning before labels

**A1**

1. Instrument: **بالقلمِ**, kasrah; attaches to **كتبت**.
2. Intended purpose: **للتعلّمِ**, kasrah; attaches to **جئت**.
3. Final limit: **حتى آخرِه**, kasrah on **آخر**; the pronoun is genitive by إضافة.
4. Partitive: **من ماءٍ**, kasratayn; **من** is شبيهة بالزائد.
5. Exhaustive scope in a question: **من مزيدٍ**, kasratayn; **مزيد** is مرفوع محلًّا مبتدأ despite its visible genitive.

**A2**

1. **مَا رَأَيْتُهُ مُنْذُ اليَوْمِ**: جرّ obligatory for the current period.
2. **مَا رَأَيْتُهُ مُذْ يَوْمَانِ**: رفع preferred for the elapsed past duration; understand **مذ مضى يومان**. The genitive alternative is not prohibited.
3. **لَيْسَ الطَّالِبُ بِمُجْتَهِدٍ**: جرّ in visible form because of additional bā, نصب in predicate position.
4. **رُبَّ طَالِبٍ مُجْتَهِدٍ لَقِيتُهُ**: **طالب** is genitive by **ربّ**; **مجتهد** follows its visible genitive as a description. The indefinite noun and description meet the taught construction.

### B. Repair the analysis

**B1**

1. **البيتِ** is genitive by **إلى**. The whole phrase’s place relationship does not replace the noun’s internal case.
2. Before a complete clause, **منذ** is a noun, **اسم مضاف**; the clause has محل جرّ بالإضافة. It does not govern only the clause’s first word.
3. **ربّ** can express many or few; **القرينة** decides.
4. In **هل زيد بقائم**, bā is additional before the predicate. There is no writing meaning and no attachment to an invented writing verb.
5. For the intended halfway point, use **إلى نصفه**. In the taught time-extent construction, **حتى** requires the end part or something connected to the end.

### C. Connected reading

**C1**

**خَرَجَ خَالِدٌ مِنَ البَيْتِ. سَارَ إِلَى المَسْجِدِ. جَلَسَ فِيهِ لِلتَّعَلُّمِ. كَتَبَ بِالقَلَمِ. عَادَ إِلَى البَيْتِ بِاللَّيْلِ.**

Khalid left the house, walked to the mosque, sat there to learn, wrote with the pen, and returned home at night.

| Phrase | Meaning and role | Attachment |
|---|---|---|
| من البيت | Starting point in place؛ مفعول فيه غير صريح | خرج |
| إلى المسجد | Endpoint in place؛ مفعول فيه غير صريح | سار |
| فيه | Location؛ مفعول فيه غير صريح | جلس |
| للتعلّم | Intended purpose؛ مفعول له غير صريح | جلس |
| بالقلم | Instrument؛ آلة | كتب |
| إلى البيت | Endpoint in place؛ مفعول فيه غير صريح | عاد |
| بالليل | Time؛ مفعول فيه غير صريح | عاد |

All the listed prepositions are original in these uses. **الهاء** in **فيه** refers to **المسجد**. The implicit subject of each verb after the first is **هو**, referring to Khalid.

Full tarkīb of **جَلَسَ فِيهِ لِلتَّعَلُّمِ**:

- **جلسَ**: فعل ماضٍ مبني على الفتح، لا محل له من الإعراب.
- Its subject: ضمير مستتر تقديره هو، في محل رفع فاعل، يعود إلى خالد.
- **في**: حرف جرّ أصليّ للظرفية؛ **الهاء**: ضمير متصل مبني على الكسر في محل جرّ بفي، يعود إلى المسجد.
- **فيه**: جارّ ومجرور متعلّق بجلس، مفعول فيه غير صريح للمكان؛ ظرف لغو.
- **اللام**: حرف جرّ أصليّ للتعليل؛ **التعلّمِ**: اسم مجرور باللام وعلامة جرّه الكسرة الظاهرة.
- **للتعلّم**: جارّ ومجرور متعلّق بجلس، مفعول له غير صريح.
- The independent utterance is a verbal sentence with no case position as a whole.

**بالقلم** identifies what the writing was performed with; **بالليل** identifies when the returning took place. Their common spelling does not make their relationships identical.

**C2**

**مَا رَأَيْتُ الضَّيْفَ مُنْذُ يَوْمِ الجُمُعَةِ. هَلْ هُوَ بِمَرِيضٍ؟ لَيْسَ الضَّيْفُ بِمَرِيضٍ. رُبَّ رَجُلٍ كَرِيمٍ لَقِيتُهُ، وَالضَّيْفُ كَرِيمٌ.**

I have not seen the guest since Friday. Is he ill? The guest is not ill. Many a generous man I have met, and the guest is generous.

In the first sentence, **الضيفَ** is the direct object; **يومِ** is genitive by **منذ** and is مضاف; **الجمعةِ** is مضاف إليه. **منذ يوم الجمعة** attaches to **رأيت**, expressing the starting point of the elapsed period. The action is past and negated.

In **هل هو بمريضٍ**, **هو** is مبتدأ in محل رفع, referring to the guest. Bā is additional; **مريضٍ** is **خبر مجرور لفظًا، مرفوع محلًّا**. In **ليس الضيفُ بمريضٍ**, **الضيفُ** is اسم ليس, while **مريضٍ** is **خبر ليس مجرور لفظًا، منصوب محلًّا**. The visible kasrah is the same; the predicate positions differ.

**ربّ رجلٍ كريمٍ لقيته**:

- **ربّ**: حرف جرّ شبيه بالزائد للتكثير؛ لا متعلّق له.
- **رجلٍ**: مبتدأ مجرور لفظًا بربّ، مرفوع محلًّا.
- **كريمٍ**: نعت مجرور بالكسرة.
- **لقيتُ**: past verb fixed on sukūn; **التاء**: subject, the speaker, in محل رفع.
- **الهاء**: object in محل نصب, referring back to **رجل**.
- **لقيته**: جملة فعلية في محل رفع خبر.

In **والضيفُ كريمٌ**, the wāw links another assertion. **الضيفُ** is مبتدأ مرفوع بالضمة; **كريمٌ** is خبر مرفوع بالضمة. **ربّ** governs its own indefinite complement **رجل**, not the nouns of this new assertion. The speaker’s **تُ** in **رأيتُ / لقيتُ** refers to the speaker; **هو** refers to the guest, whereas the object **ه** in **لقيته** resumes the man introduced with **ربّ**.

### D. Read grammatical Arabic

1. “The complement of the comparative kāf must be an overt noun.” Example: **كتبت كالمعلمِ**. An attached pronoun is not an interchangeable substitute in this prepositional construction.
2. “An additional preposition does not require an attachment.” Example: **ليس زيد بقائمٍ**. **قائم** is the predicate; do not invent a separate governor for a bā phrase.
3. “Partitive min supplies a specific meaning.” Example: **عندي من ماءٍ** expresses some water; it is not merely emphatic.
4. “Genitive after mundhu is obligatory for the present period and preferred for the past.” Compare **منذ اليومِ** with **منذ يومِ الجمعةِ**. Keep **واجب** and **راجح** distinct.
5. “The phrase’s function differs according to meaning and contextual evidence.” Compare **كتبت بالقلم** with **سافرت بالليل**, or the two referents and readings of **على حبّه**.

### E. Independent tarkīb

**E1.1 — كَتَبَ الضَّيْفُ بِقَلَمِ المُعَلِّمِ**

- **كتبَ**: فعل ماضٍ مبني على الفتح، لا محل له من الإعراب.
- **الضيفُ**: فاعل مرفوع بالضمة الظاهرة.
- **الباء**: حرف جرّ أصليّ للاستعانة، مبني لا محل له من الإعراب.
- **قلمِ**: اسم مجرور بالباء بالكسرة، وهو مضاف.
- **المعلمِ**: مضاف إليه مجرور بالكسرة.
- **بقلم المعلم**: جارّ ومجرور متعلّق بكتب، بمعنى الآلة. The preposition directly governs **قلم**, while إضافة governs **المعلم**.
- The sentence is an independent verbal utterance, with no case position as a whole.

**E1.2 — سِرْتُ مِنَ المَسْجِدِ إِلَى السُّوقِ**

- **سرتُ**: فعل ماضٍ مبني على السكون لاتصاله بتاء الفاعل؛ **التاء**: ضمير متصل مبني على الضم في محل رفع فاعل.
- **من**: حرف جرّ أصليّ لابتداء الغاية؛ **المسجدِ**: اسم مجرور بمن بالكسرة.
- **إلى**: حرف جرّ أصليّ لانتهاء الغاية؛ **السوقِ**: اسم مجرور بإلى بالكسرة.
- Both phrases attach to **سرت**, expressing place through complementary relations: the journey’s beginning and endpoint. Each is **مفعول فيه غير صريح** in meaning. Their noun endings remain genitive.
- The independent verbal sentence has no case position as a whole.

**E1.3 — لَيْسَ الرَّجُلُ بِقَائِمٍ**

- **ليس**: فعل ماضٍ ناقص جامد للنفي، مبني على الفتح، لا محل له من الإعراب.
- **الرجلُ**: اسم ليس مرفوع بالضمة.
- **الباء**: حرف جرّ زائد للتأكيد، لا متعلّق له.
- **قائمٍ**: خبر ليس، مجرور لفظًا بالباء، منصوب محلًّا.
- This is an independent sentence with no outer case position; the predicate position belongs to **قائم**, not to a new original-preposition complement.

**E1.4 — رُبَّ ضَيْفٍ كَرِيمٍ لَقِيتُهُ**

- **ربّ**: حرف جرّ شبيه بالزائد، للتكثير أو التقليل بحسب القرينة؛ لا متعلّق له. Without further context, either quantity reading is possible.
- **ضيفٍ**: مبتدأ مجرور لفظًا بربّ، مرفوع محلًّا.
- **كريمٍ**: نعت مجرور بالكسرة, agreeing with the noun’s visible genitive.
- **لقيتُ**: فعل ماضٍ مبني على السكون؛ **التاء**: ضمير في محل رفع فاعل.
- **الهاء**: ضمير متصل مبني على الضم في محل نصب مفعول به، يعود إلى الضيف.
- **لقيته**: جملة فعلية في محل رفع خبر. The full utterance is an independent nominal predication with no outer case position.

**E1.5 — مَا رَأَيْتُهُ مُنْذُ يَوْمَيْنِ**

- **ما**: حرف نفي، لا محل له من الإعراب.
- **رأيتُ**: فعل ماضٍ مبني على السكون؛ **التاء**: ضمير مبني على الضم في محل رفع فاعل.
- **الهاء**: ضمير متصل مبني على الضم في محل نصب مفعول به. Its specific referent requires context.
- **منذ**: حرف جرّ أصليّ, expressing the elapsed duration.
- **يومينِ**: اسم مجرور بمنذ، وعلامة جرّه الياء لأنه مثنّى.
- **منذ يومين**: جارّ ومجرور متعلّق برأيت، مفعول فيه غير صريح للزمان. The governing action is past and negated; the genitive is the preferred past-time construction after **منذ**.
- The whole independent sentence has no case position.

**E2 — One possible pair**

**كَتَبْتُ بِالقَلَمِ. لَيْسَ الطِّفْلُ بِنَائِمٍ.**

In the first, **القلمِ** is genitive by original bā; the phrase attaches to **كتبت** and expresses an instrument. In the second, **نائمٍ** is genitive in visible form by additional bā but accusative in position as خبر ليس; there is no original-preposition attachment. Both show kasrah, but their structures differ.
