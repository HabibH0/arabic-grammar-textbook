# Module 6: Particles Governing Verbs

الحُرُوفُ العَامِلَةُ فِي الفِعْلِ

## Before you begin

You should recognise **فعل، فاعل، مفعول به، مبتدأ، خبر، مضاف إليه**, attached and implicit pronouns, and the difference between **إعراب** and **بناء**. Brief reminders below supply the verb forms needed here.

Your task is to explain **what governs a verb, what the verb itself governs, and how the whole sentence fits together**. Learn the Arabic terms alongside the rules. Attempt each lesson's exercises before opening the separate answers.

Early examples include vowels and translations. Later exercises remove this support. Supply grammatical endings for connected speech; do not replace them with pause-endings. Unless a task asks about a recognised alternative, use the principal analysis taught in the lesson.

## Lesson 1: Recognising the verb's ending

### Core rule and terminology

An inflected **فِعْل مُضَارِع** (*fiʿl muḍāriʿ*, present/future verb) has three moods:

| Term | Function here | Example |
|---|---|---|
| رَفْع — مَرْفُوع | Indicative: no governing ناصب or جازم | يَكْتُبُ — he writes |
| نَصْب — مَنْصُوب | Subjunctive: governed by a نَاصِب | لَنْ يَكْتُبَ — he will not write |
| جَزْم — مَجْزُوم | Jussive: governed by a جَازِم | لَمْ يَكْتُبْ — he did not write |

Verbs do not take **جرّ**. The English labels name grammatical moods; they do not supply a single translation for every use.

### The small amount of ṣarf you need

The prefixes in **يكتب، تكتب، أكتب، نكتب** help identify the subject: he; she or you; I; we. The full context decides between “she” and “you.” With no expressed subject, identify the appropriate **ضمير مستتر**, implicit pronoun.

The **أَفْعَال خَمْسَة**, five verb patterns, are **يفعلان، تفعلان، يفعلون، تفعلون، تفعلين**. Their attached **ألف الاثنين، واو الجماعة، ياء المخاطبة** are subject pronouns. The final **نون الإعراب** marks رفع; it is not the subject.

| Verb type | رفع | نصب | جزم |
|---|---|---|---|
| صَحِيح الآخِر: final consonant is sound | يَكْتُبُ | لَنْ يَكْتُبَ | لَمْ يَكْتُبْ |
| الأفعال الخمسة | يَكْتُبُونَ | لَنْ يَكْتُبُوا | لَمْ يَكْتُبُوا |
| مُعْتَلّ الآخِر: final و | يَدْعُو | لَنْ يَدْعُوَ | لَمْ يَدْعُ |
| معتلّ الآخر: final ي | يَرْمِي | لَنْ يَرْمِيَ | لَمْ يَرْمِ |
| معتلّ الآخر: final ا/ى | يَسْعَى | لَنْ يَسْعَى | لَمْ يَسْعَ |

**يدعو** = calls; **يرمي** = throws; **يسعى** = strives.

- A sound ending has **ضمة، فتحة، سكون**. Jussive sukūn is also described as **حذف الحركة**, removal of the vowel.
- The five patterns have **ثبوت النون** in رفع and **حذف النون** in both نصب and جزم. In **يكتبوا**, the و remains the subject; the final ا is written but not pronounced.
- Final و/ي have an estimated ḍammah in رفع, a visible fatḥah in نصب, and **حذف حرف العلة**, deletion of the weak letter, in جزم.
- Final alif has an estimated ḍammah or fatḥah; جزم deletes the alif. **يسعى** therefore needs context to distinguish رفع from نصب.

### Worked tarkīb

**لَنْ يَكْتُبُوا الرِّسَالَةَ.** — They will not write the letter.

- **لن**: حرف نفي ونصب واستقبال; a particle of negation, نصب, and future reference.
- **يكتبوا**: فعل مضارع منصوب بلن، وعلامة نصبه حذف النون لأنه من الأفعال الخمسة.
- **واو الجماعة**: ضمير متصل في محل رفع فاعل.
- **الرسالةَ**: مفعول به منصوب بالفتحة.

The verb is **معمول** of لن, but **عامل** in its subject and object. نصب on the verb does not make its subject منصوبًا.

## Lesson 2: Four explicit particles of نصب

### أَنْ، كَيْ، لَنْ، إِذَنْ

| Particle | Meaning and grammatical description | Example |
|---|---|---|
| أَنْ | حرف مصدر ونصب; turns its verbal construction into a noun-like unit | أُرِيدُ أَنْ أَفْهَمَ — I want to understand |
| كَيْ | حرف مصدر للتعليل; introduces a purpose | أَقْرَأُ كَيْ أَفْهَمَ — I read in order to understand |
| لَنْ | لتأكيد النفي في المستقبل; emphatic future negation | لَنْ أَتْرُكَ الدَّرْسَ — I will not abandon the lesson |
| إِذَنْ | حرف جواب; introduces a response or consequence | إِذَنْ أُكْرِمَكَ — Then I shall honour you |

With **أَنْ**, the **مصدر مؤوّل**, interpreted verbal noun, has a role in the larger sentence. **أريد أن أفهم** can be understood structurally as **أريد الفهمَ**. The verb inside the construction still has its own subject.

With **كَيْ المصدرية الناصبة**, a purpose **لام الجرّ** is expressed or understood: **أقرأ لِكَيْ أفهمَ** or **أقرأ كَيْ أفهمَ**. In this analysis, **كي أفهم** is a مصدر مؤوّل governed by the expressed or understood لام. **كي** also has a prepositional use; do not combine two different analyses of the same occurrence.

**كي** characteristically expresses a genuine purpose. Purpose لام has a wider range, including a cause or an eventual outcome. Translate the relationship in context.

**لن** does not by itself mean “never for all eternity.” Permanent negation requires a contextual indication, **قرينة**.

### When إِذَنْ governs

Three conditions must hold:

1. It begins its response: **في صدر الكلام**.
2. The following verb refers to the future: **فعل مستقبل**.
3. Nothing separates it from the verb, except an oath: **القَسَم**.

Someone says: **سَأَزُورُكَ.** — I shall visit you.

| Response | Result |
|---|---|
| إِذَنْ أُكْرِمَكَ | نصب: all three conditions hold |
| أَنَا إِذَنْ أُكْرِمُكَ | رفع: إذن is not at the beginning |
| إِذَنْ أَنَا أُكْرِمُكَ | رفع: أنا separates إذن from the verb |
| إِذَنْ وَاللهِ أُكْرِمَكَ | نصب: an intervening oath is allowed |

If the meaning is present rather than future, إذن does not govern نصب. Following **وَ** or **فَ**, governing and non-governing usages are both possible; **إهمال**, non-government, is more common. A further attested usage leaves إذن non-governing even when the three conditions hold. Recognise it when reading; use the principal governing pattern in exercises. You may also encounter the spelling **إِذًا**.

### Worked tarkīb

**أُرِيدُ أَنْ يَقْرَأَ خَالِدٌ الكِتَابَ.** — I want Khalid to read the book.

- **أريدُ**: مضارع مرفوع بالضمة؛ فاعله ضمير مستتر تقديره أنا.
- **أن**: حرف مصدر ونصب.
- **يقرأَ**: مضارع منصوب بأن، وعلامة نصبه الفتحة.
- **خالدٌ**: فاعل يقرأ مرفوع بالضمة.
- **الكتابَ**: مفعول به ليقرأ منصوب بالفتحة.
- **أن يقرأ خالد الكتاب**: مصدر مؤوّل في محل نصب مفعول به لأريد; roughly, “Khalid's reading of the book.”

There are two subjects and two object relationships at different levels.

## Lesson 3: Which أَنْ, and when may it be understood?

### أَنْ المصدرية and أَنْ المخفّفة

Do not assume that every **أن** makes the next verb منصوبًا.

In these complement constructions, examine the preceding **فعل قلب**, a verb of knowing or thinking:

| Preceding meaning | Analysis |
|---|---|
| يَقِين — certainty, as with علم | أن مخفّفة من الثقيلة; lightened from أنّ |
| ظَنّ — supposition, as with حسب | Either مخفّفة or مصدرية, according to the construction |
| No preceding فعل قلب of this kind | The أن in the infinitival constructions taught here is مصدرية |

**عَلِمْتُ أَنْ سَيَحْضُرُ خَالِدٌ.** — I knew that Khalid would attend.

Here **أن** is **مخفّفة**. Its اسم is an omitted pronoun, **ضمير الشأن**; the clause **سيحضر خالد** is its خبر. **يحضرُ** is مرفوع: this أن does not govern the verb in نصب. **سـ** marks future reference.

After supposition, compare:

- **حَسِبْتُ أَلَّا يَحْضُرَ خَالِدٌ**: أن مصدرية ناصبة; لا is negative.
- **حَسِبْتُ أَلَّا يَحْضُرُ خَالِدٌ**: أن مخفّفة; its اسم is omitted, and **لا يحضر خالد** is its خبر.

Both mean “I thought Khalid would not attend”; the internal construction differs. **أَلَّا** here represents **أَنْ + لَا**. Neither is the conditional **إِنْ**.

### أَنْ مضمرة جوازًا

**إِضْمَار** means that a word is understood without being spoken or written. **جَوَازًا** means that expressing it is also permitted in this construction.

There are two regular settings:

1. After a prepositional **لام**, where the construction does not require obligatory concealment, and where أن is not joined to negative لا.
2. After a conjunction joining the interpreted verbal noun to an explicit noun, **اسم صريح غير مؤوّل**.

**جِئْتُ لِأَتَعَلَّمَ.** — I came to learn.

Expand it: **جئتُ لِأَنْ أَتَعَلَّمَ**. The لام governs the مصدر مؤوّل in جرّ; understood أن governs **أتعلمَ** in نصب. If negative لا follows, أن is expressed: **جئتُ لِئَلَّا أَتَأَخَّرَ**, “I came so as not to be late.” **لئلّا = لِـ + أَنْ + لَا**.

**قِرَاءَتُكَ وَتَفْهَمَ خَيْرٌ مِنْ قِرَاءَةٍ بِغَيْرِ فَهْمٍ.**

Your reading together with understanding is better than reading without understanding.

Here **وتفهمَ** represents **وأن تفهمَ**. The مصدر مؤوّل, “your understanding,” is coordinated with the explicit noun **قراءتُك**. The conjunction alone is not a general-purpose ناصب. This pattern is for recognition; ordinary **قراءتك وفهمك** expresses the relationship more simply.

### Worked analysis: two levels of government

In **جئتُ لأتعلّمَ**:

- **جئتُ**: past verb fixed on sukūn; تُ is في محل رفع فاعل.
- **لـ**: لام التعليل، حرف جرّ.
- **أتعلّمَ**: مضارع منصوب بأن مضمرة جوازًا؛ فاعله ضمير مستتر تقديره أنا.
- **أن أتعلّم**: مصدر مؤوّل في محل جرّ باللام.
- The prepositional construction attaches to **جئت**, explaining its purpose.

### A meaning distinction to notice

When the context makes a bare أن-construction causal, it can give an already realised reason; a purpose لام alone commonly points to an intended result. Expressed **لأنْ** can allow either reading according to context. These are contextual tendencies, not a rule that every bare أن refers to a past event: **أريد أن أتعلمَ** plainly concerns something wanted.

## Lesson 4: أَنْ that must remain unexpressed

### The first three settings

**أَنْ مضمرة وجوبًا** means that أن is understood but must remain unexpressed in the construction being analysed. Five settings occur here; learn three now and two in Lesson 5.

| Setting | Condition or meaning | Example |
|---|---|---|
| حتّى | The event is subsequent to the relevant earlier time | أَنْتَظِرُ حَتَّى يَرْجِعَ خَالِدٌ — I wait until Khalid returns |
| لام الجُحُود | Emphatic denial after a negated كان-construction | مَا كَانَ خَالِدٌ لِيَكْذِبَ — Khalid was not one to lie |
| أو بمعنى إلى أن | A limit: “until” | لَأَجْتَهِدَنَّ أَوْ أَفْهَمَ — I shall certainly strive until I understand |
| أو بمعنى إلّا أن | An exception: “unless” | لَأَلْزَمَنَّكَ أَوْ تَقْضِيَ حَقِّي — I shall certainly stay with you unless you discharge what you owe me |

**جحود** = emphatic denial; **إلى أن** = until; **إلّا أن** = unless. In the last two examples, initial لَـ and final **نّ** reinforce an undertaking. They do not make the second verb منصوبًا. Detailed oath formation is not needed to recognise **أو** here.

### حتّى: future relative to what?

If the event after حتّى is future relative to the time of speaking, نصب is required in this construction: **سَأَنْتَظِرُ حَتَّى يَرْجِعَ خَالِدٌ**.

If it was future only relative to an earlier event, نصب and رفع readings can both occur. نصب presents the later event as an anticipated endpoint; رفع can present it as a realised scene. Do not mechanically make every مضارع after حتّى منصوبًا.

The analysis used here is **منصوب بأن مضمرة وجوبًا بعد حتّى**. Another recognised analysis treats حتّى itself as the ناصب, without estimating أن. Keep the two analyses separate.

### Worked comparison: two lams

**جَاءَ خَالِدٌ لِيَتَعَلَّمَ.** — Khalid came to learn.

**ليتعلّمَ** expresses purpose: لام التعليل; أن is **مضمرة جوازًا**.

**مَا كَانَ خَالِدٌ لِيَكْذِبَ.** — Khalid was not one to lie.

- **ما**: حرف نفي.
- **كان**: فعل ماضٍ ناقص؛ **خالدٌ**: اسم كان مرفوع.
- **لـ**: لام الجحود؛ reinforces the denial.
- **يكذبَ**: مضارع منصوب بأن مضمرة وجوبًا؛ فاعله ضمير مستتر تقديره هو، يعود إلى خالد.

For the larger construction, two analyses are recognised. One treats the لام as **زائدة للتوكيد**, reinforcing the denied predicate. The other treats it as **أصلية**: the مصدر مؤوّل is governed by لام in جرّ, and the prepositional construction relates to an omitted خبر كان such as **قاصدًا**, “intending.” Do not combine these two accounts. Both preserve the verb-level analysis with obligatory understood أن.

The negative كان-construction and the intended denial distinguish this لام from an ordinary purpose لام.

## Lesson 5: Accompaniment, consequence, and changing meanings

### واو الصرف and فاء السببية

The remaining two settings for obligatory understood أن are:

- **واو الصَّرْف**, also **واو المعيّة** here: “together with,” following negation or a request concerning an action.
- **فاء السَّبَبِيّة**: introduces a consequence, following negation or a request concerning an action.

**الصرف** here means turning the verb away from the ending another surface pattern might suggest, toward نصب with understood أن. It does not mean morphology in this term.

### One sentence, three meanings

| Sentence | Analysis of تكتب | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| لَا تَتَكَلَّمْ وَتَكْتُبَ | منصوب بأن مضمرة وجوبًا بعد واو المعية | Do not combine speaking and writing; either separately is permitted |
| لَا تَتَكَلَّمْ وَتَكْتُبْ | مجزوم بالعطف على تتكلم | Do not speak and do not write; each is prohibited |
| لَا تَتَكَلَّمْ وَتَكْتُبُ | مرفوع على الاستئناف | Do not speak; you write. The second is a separate statement |

In all three, **تتكلمْ** is مجزوم by prohibitive لا. The second verb's ending determines whether the relationship is accompaniment, coordination, or a fresh statement: **معيّة، عطف، استئناف**.

The same distinction matters after negation: not combining two acts is different from denying each act separately.

### What counts as طلب?

Learn these terms by recognising what the speaker is doing. In each example below, the second action is presented as a consequence, and its verb is منصوب بأن مضمرة وجوبًا after فاء السببية.

| Term | Function | Example |
|---|---|---|
| أَمْر | Command | اِجْتَهِدْ فَتَنْجَحَ — Work hard, so that you succeed |
| نَهْي | Prohibition | لَا تُهْمِلْ فَتَنْدَمَ — Do not neglect your duty and consequently regret it |
| اِسْتِفْهَام | Question | هَلْ تُسَاعِدُنِي فَأَشْكُرَكَ؟ — Will you help me, so that I may thank you? |
| تَمَنٍّ | Wish | لَيْتَكَ تَحْضُرُ فَأَفْرَحَ — If only you would attend, so that I might rejoice |
| تَرَجٍّ | Hope | لَعَلَّكَ تَجْتَهِدُ فَتَنْجَحَ — Perhaps you will work hard and so succeed |
| عَرْض | Gentle invitation | أَلَا تَزُورُنَا فَنُكْرِمَكَ؟ — Will you visit us, so that we may honour you? |
| تَحْضِيض | Strong urging | هَلَّا تَجْتَهِدُ فَتَنْجَحَ؟ — Why not work hard, so that you succeed? |

Negative statements also qualify: **لَا يُهْمِلُ خَالِدٌ فَيَنْدَمَ**, “Khalid does not neglect his duty and consequently regret it.” The first verb here is مرفوع after negative لا; the second has نصب in the consequence construction. This differs from prohibitive **لا تهملْ**.

Neither every و nor every ف conceals أن. The intended relationship and the preceding negation or طلب are essential. Ordinary coordination can retain رفع: **يقرأُ خالدٌ ويكتبُ**.

### Worked tarkīb

**اِجْتَهِدْ فَتَفْهَمَ الدَّرْسَ.** — Work hard, so that you understand the lesson.

- **اجتهدْ**: فعل أمر مبني على السكون؛ فاعله ضمير مستتر تقديره أنت.
- **فـ**: فاء السببية، مسبوقة بطلب.
- **تفهمَ**: مضارع منصوب بأن مضمرة وجوبًا؛ فاعله ضمير مستتر تقديره أنت.
- **الدرسَ**: مفعول به منصوب بالفتحة.

At the construction level, **أن تفهم الدرس** is a مصدر مؤوّل coordinated with a **مصدر متصيّد**, an understood verbal noun recovered from the preceding command: “striving, followed by understanding.” **متصيّد** here means inferred from the preceding words.

### A limit on inventing patterns

Some expressions have نصب by an unexpressed أن outside these regular settings: **إضمار أن سماعًا**. **سماعًا** means established by attested usage, and **لا يقاس عليها** means that new examples should not be formed by analogy.

Recognise **مُرْهُ يَحْفِرَهَا**, “Tell him to dig it,” where **يحفرَ** has this exceptional نصب. Do not infer that any verb after an imperative may take نصب without a particle.

## Lesson 6: Particles governing one verb in جزم

### Four particles, four functions

| Particle | Description | Example |
|---|---|---|
| لَمْ | حرف نفي وجزم وقلب | لَمْ يَحْضُرْ خَالِدٌ — Khalid did not attend |
| لَمَّا | Negates up to now, with expected occurrence | لَمَّا يَحْضُرْ خَالِدٌ — Khalid has not yet attended |
| لام الأَمْر | Requests that an action be performed | لِيَكْتُبْ خَالِدٌ — Let Khalid write |
| لا الناهية | Prohibits an action | لَا تَكْذِبْ — Do not lie |

**قَلْب** means changing the time-reference: لم and negative لمّا take a present-form verb but give past negation. **تَوَقُّع الحُصُول**, expectation of occurrence, distinguishes لمّا: “not yet,” not simply “did not.”

After فـ, command لام is commonly read with sukūn: **فَلْيَكْتُبْ**. It still governs جزم.

### Do not confuse identical-looking particles

- **لا يَكْذِبُ خالدٌ**: negative statement; لا النافية does not govern this verb.
- **لا تَكْذِبْ**: prohibition; لا الناهية governs جزم.
- **جئتُ لِأَكْتُبَ**: purpose لام, with understood أن.
- **لِيَكْتُبْ خالدٌ**: command لام, governing the verb directly in جزم.

Meaning and construction identify the لام or لا; their shape alone is insufficient.

### Worked tarkīb: a deleted weak letter

**لَمْ يَرْمِ الوَلَدُ الحَجَرَ.** — The boy did not throw the stone.

- **لم**: حرف نفي وجزم وقلب.
- **يرمِ**: مضارع مجزوم بلم، وعلامة جزمه حذف حرف العلة؛ أصله يرمي.
- **الولدُ**: فاعل مرفوع بالضمة.
- **الحجرَ**: مفعول به منصوب بالفتحة.

The remaining kasrah is not جرّ. It accompanies deletion of the final ي.

### Fixed forms still have grammatical positions

With **نون النسوة**, the subject pronoun for a feminine plural, the verb is fixed on sukūn: **يَكْتُبْنَ**. This ن is not نون الإعراب and must not be deleted.

**الطَّالِبَاتُ لَمْ يَكْتُبْنَ.** — The female students did not write.

**يكتبنَ**: فعل مضارع مبني على السكون في محل جزم بلم. **نون النسوة**: ضمير متصل في محل رفع فاعل. The verbal clause is the خبر of **الطالباتُ**.

Compare **الطالباتُ لن يكتبْنَ**: the same fixed form is now **في محل نصب**. **البناء** describes its fixed form; **المحلّ** describes its governed position.

## Lesson 7: Particles governing a condition and its response

### شرط and جزاء

**إِنْ** and **إِذْمَا** are the two conditional particles here that govern two verbs in جزم. The first is **فِعْل الشَّرْط**; the second is **جَوَاب الشَّرْط**, also **الجَزَاء**.

**إِنْ تَجْتَهِدْ تَنْجَحْ.** — If you work hard, you will succeed.

The response is tied to the condition. This is different from two unrelated jussive verbs.

### Worked tarkīb

**إِنْ تَفْتَحْ البَابَ يَدْخُلْ خَالِدٌ.** — If you open the door, Khalid will enter.

- **إن**: حرف شرط جازم يجزم فعلين.
- **تفتحْ**: فعل مضارع مجزوم بإن، وعلامة جزمه السكون، وهو فعل الشرط؛ فاعله ضمير مستتر تقديره أنت.
- **البابَ**: مفعول به منصوب بالفتحة.
- **يدخلْ**: مضارع مجزوم، جواب الشرط، وعلامة جزمه السكون.
- **خالدٌ**: فاعل يدخل مرفوع بالضمة.

**إذما** means إن: **إِذْمَا تَجْتَهِدْ تَنْجَحْ**. Do not confuse it with **إذن**, a particle of response that may govern نصب.

### Four combinations of verb forms

| شرط + جواب | Example | Ending to explain |
|---|---|---|
| مضارع + مضارع | إِنْ تَجْتَهِدْ تَنْجَحْ | Both visibly مجزوم |
| ماضٍ + ماضٍ | إِنْ رَجَعْتَ رَجَعْتُ | Both fixed; each في محل جزم |
| مضارع + ماضٍ | إِنْ تَرْجِعْ رَجَعْتُ | First visibly مجزوم; second fixed, في محل جزم |
| ماضٍ + مضارع | إِنْ رَجَعْتَ أَرْجِعْ / أَرْجِعُ | Both جزم and رفع are permitted for the مضارع response |

**رجعتَ** = you returned; **رجعتُ** = I returned. Both are مبني على السكون because an attached, vowelled subject pronoun follows. Their fixed form does not prevent a conditional grammatical position.

A past verb in a future-oriented condition need not describe an event already completed. It can present the hypothetical act as one occurrence or as a whole. A مضارع can present repeated or unfolding action. These are contextual distinctions, not instructions to translate every past form as English past tense.

### Meaning: إِنْ and إِذَا

**إن** can frame a possible, doubtful, supposed, or even impossible condition. Introducing a supposition does not assert that it actually occurs. **إذا** characteristically presents an event treated as certain to occur; it is not one of the two jussive particles taught here. Occasionally إن is used for an otherwise certain event to suggest its infrequency. Avoid the absolute rule “إن always means uncertainty.”

### إِمَّا: reinforced إن

**ما الزائدة للتوكيد** may follow إن: **إنْ + ما → إمّا**. Here **زائدة** means added for grammatical emphasis, not meaningless. The condition verb then takes **نون التوكيد**.

**إِمَّا تَجْتَهِدَنَّ فَسَتَنْجَحُ.** — If you do indeed work hard, then you will succeed.

In **تجتهدَنَّ**, the verb is **مبني على الفتح في محل جزم**, with implicit subject أنت. The directly attached نون is for emphasis, not a subject or نون الإعراب. The response begins with linking فـ; its role is the next lesson's topic.

## Lesson 8: Responses with فـ, and conditions left unspoken

### فاء الجواب

A conditional response sometimes needs **فاء الجواب**, also **الفاء الرابطة للجواب**. It links the whole response to the condition. It is different from فاء السببية followed by understood أن.

| Response | Example | Internal analysis |
|---|---|---|
| Nominal sentence | إِنْ تَجْتَهِدْ فَأَنْتَ نَاجِحٌ | أنت: مبتدأ؛ ناجح: خبر مرفوع |
| Command | إِنْ حَضَرَ خَالِدٌ فَأَكْرِمْهُ | أكرمْ: فعل أمر مبني على السكون |
| Prohibition | إِنْ حَضَرَ الضَّيْفُ فَلَا تُهْمِلْهُ | تهملْ is governed by لا الناهية |
| Future negation | إِنْ تَجْتَهِدْ فَلَنْ تَنْدَمَ | تندمَ is governed by لن |
| سـ or سوف | إِنْ تَجْتَهِدْ فَسَوْفَ تَنْجَحُ | تنجحُ remains مرفوع |

With linking فـ, إن does not directly govern the response verb's ending. The **whole response clause** is **في محل جزم جواب الشرط**; the words inside it follow their own governors.

Also recognise required linking فـ with a response whose verb is جامد; an affirmative past response introduced by expressed or understood قد; a past response negated by ما; or a response beginning with كأنما or another conditional construction. These are recognition categories; the examples above are the production patterns for this lesson.

With an ordinary affirmative مضارع response lacking سـ/سوف, فـ may be present or absent: **إن تجتهدْ تنجحْ** / **إن تجتهدْ فتنجحُ**. The ending changes with the structure. A response negated by ordinary لا also permits both linking choices. Do not insert فـ mechanically before a bare past response without قد, or before a لم-response treated as past in meaning: **إن حضرتَ حضرتُ**; **إن خرجتَ لم أخرجْ**.

### Worked full tarkīb

**إِنْ تَجْتَهِدْ فَلَنْ تَنْدَمَ.** — If you work hard, you will not regret it.

- **إن**: حرف شرط جازم.
- **تجتهدْ**: مضارع مجزوم بإن، وعلامة جزمه السكون؛ فعل الشرط؛ فاعله ضمير مستتر تقديره أنت.
- **فـ**: رابطة لجواب الشرط؛ لا محل لها من الإعراب.
- **لن**: حرف نفي ونصب واستقبال.
- **تندمَ**: مضارع منصوب بلن، وعلامة نصبه الفتحة؛ فاعله ضمير مستتر تقديره أنت.
- **لن تندم**: جملة في محل جزم جواب الشرط، مقترنة بالفاء.

The verb's نصب and the clause's محل جزم describe different levels. A response directly governed without a linking particle, such as **تنجحْ** in **إن تجتهدْ تنجحْ**, does not acquire this separate clause-position merely because it is a response.

### إن المقدّرة: a result after طلب

A مضارع intended as the result of a request can be مجزوم through an understood conditional construction: **إن مقدّرة**. The result relationship is essential.

**اِجْتَهِدْ تَنْجَحْ.** — Work hard and you will succeed.

Recover: **إن تجتهدْ تنجحْ**. In this analysis, **تنجحْ** is مجزوم as the result of the understood condition, also called **جواب الطلب**. **اجتهدْ** itself remains an imperative, مبني.

| Preceding expression | Example | Recovered condition |
|---|---|---|
| Command | اُدْخُلْ تَسْتَرِحْ | إن تدخلْ تسترحْ |
| Question | هَلْ تَزُورُنَا نُكْرِمْكَ؟ | إن تزرْنا نكرمْك |
| Wish | لَيْتَكَ عِنْدِي أَخْدِمْكَ | إن كنتَ عندي أخدمْك |
| Gentle invitation | أَلَا تَنْزِلُ بِنَا تُصِبْ خَيْرًا؟ | إن تنزلْ بنا تصبْ خيرًا |

**تسترح** = you rest, from **تستريح**; **تزر** = you visit, from **تزور**; **تُصِبْ** = you obtain, from **تُصِيبُ**. These verbs lose an internal weak letter in the jussive form shown. Their jussive sign is the sukūn on the final consonant; do not call that change **حذف حرف العلة** as a mood-sign, which applies to a final weak letter.

Compare three constructions:

- **اجتهدْ تنجحْ**: implied conditional result; جزم.
- **اجتهدْ فتنجحَ**: فاء السببية and obligatory understood أن; نصب.
- **إن تجتهدْ فستنجحُ**: explicit condition and linking فـ; رفع inside the response.

Read the question's final verb as an intended conditional result. **السفر** = travel; **أخي** = my brother.

1. اضبط أواخر الأفعال، واذكر العامل وعلامة الإعراب.

### D. Diagnose the reasoning

Correct each analysis, not merely its final vowel.

1. “In **لن يكتبوا**, واو الجماعة is the sign of نصب.”
2. “Every **أن** makes the following verb منصوبًا.”
3. “Every verb after و or ف takes نصب.”
4. “**لم يرمِ** is مجرور because it ends in kasrah.”
5. “A fixed past verb cannot have a conditional grammatical position.”
6. “The verb in **إن تجتهد فلن تندمَ** must be مجزوم because it is in the response.”
7. “**لن** always means permanent negation, and **إن** always implies doubt.”

### E. Full tarkīb without prompts

1. **لن يكتب الطالب الرسالة حتى يفهم السؤال**.
2. **إن تحفظوا الدرس فلن تنسوا القاعدة**.
